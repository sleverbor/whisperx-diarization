# Caption gap review

Two of four caption-timed candidates contained genuine speech missing from the baseline. One was non-target speech; the other contained multiple or overlapping speakers. The two false candidates repeated wording already present immediately before the apparent gap, caused by caption timing drift.

A nearby-transcript duplicate check removes both false candidates and retains both genuine omissions on this small calibration set. This remains review prioritization, not an automatic transcript insertion rule.

The review also exposed an independent limitation: distant non-target speech under foreground music was missing from both the baseline and the captions. Caption comparison cannot detect material absent from both sources.
